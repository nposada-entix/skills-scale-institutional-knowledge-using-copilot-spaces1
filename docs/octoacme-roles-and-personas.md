# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, manage testing resources, and ensure software meets acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for each project phase
- Plan and manage manual and automated testing efforts
- Collaborate with developers on testability and acceptance criteria
- Identify and triage defects with severity and impact assessment
- Ensure test coverage aligns with project risk profile

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality software with minimal post-release defects
- Balance speed and quality through smart testing trade-offs
- Empower developers with feedback that drives quality improvements

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and backlog refinement
- Daily standup updates on test progress and blockers
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
QA/Testing Leads work closely with **Developers** to establish testability requirements and review test coverage, collaborate with **Product Managers** to validate acceptance criteria and UAT coordination, and coordinate with **Project Managers** on test schedule and resource planning.

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions that align with business goals and technical constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approaches
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Review technical designs and ensure adherence to standards
- Mentor developers on architecture and design patterns
- Guide decisions on technology choices and trade-offs

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, scalable system growth
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Ensure solutions meet non-functional requirements (performance, security, reliability)

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical design docs and spike investigations
- Architecture syncs with product and engineering leaders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Technical Architects engage with **Developers** in design reviews and mentoring, advise **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs, and provide input to **Project Managers** on technical complexity and risk assessment.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions by eliciting requirements, analyzing user workflows, and ensuring projects deliver business value.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirement elicitation
- Analyze current processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Define detailed functional requirements and use cases
- Validate solutions against business objectives
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT) coordination

### Goals
- Ensure solutions solve real business problems
- Reduce rework by capturing accurate requirements upfront
- Bridge communication between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Stakeholder interviews and UAT sessions
- Requirements refinement sessions with product and engineering

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Business Analysts work with **Product Managers** to refine and document requirements, collaborate with **Developers** to clarify functional specifications, and support **Project Managers** in scope definition and stakeholder communication.

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers build and maintain the systems that enable reliable, scalable deployments and operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure, monitoring, and observability
- Ensure security and compliance in deployment processes
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Optimize deployment speed and reliability

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain high system reliability and performance
- Reduce operational overhead through automation

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure planning and deployment readiness reviews
- On-call rotation and incident response
- Metrics, alerts, and observability dashboards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers partner with **Developers** on deployment pipelines and monitoring, coordinate with **Project Managers** on release schedules and rollback plans, and ensure **QA/Testing Leads** have appropriate environments for testing.

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate iterative delivery, remove blockers, and help teams continuously improve their processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Remove team blockers and facilitate cross-team collaboration
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint board health and process adherence
- Support team velocity tracking and forecasting

### Goals
- Enable predictable, sustainable delivery
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce process overhead while maintaining visibility

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- One-on-one coaching and team retrospective follow-ups
- Metrics and team health assessments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches support all team members by facilitating collaboration between **Developers**, **Product Managers**, and **Project Managers**, and help emerging roles like **QA/Testing Leads**, **Technical Architects**, **Business Analysts**, and **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers** integrate smoothly into agile workflows.
